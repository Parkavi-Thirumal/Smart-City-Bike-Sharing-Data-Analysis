# Smart-City-Bike-Sharing-Data-Analysis
## 📌 Project Overview
The Bike Sharing Station Analysis project examines bike availability, station utilization, capacity, and operational status across multiple cities. Using Power BI, the project transforms raw station data into actionable insights through data cleaning, modeling, and visualization. The analysis helps identify high-performing stations, underutilized resources, and opportunities to optimize bike distribution, network efficiency, and service accessibility.
## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze the operational performance of bike-sharing stations across multiple cities.
- Evaluate bike availability, station capacity, and utilization rates.
- Identify high-performing and underutilized stations.
- Compare city-wise network coverage and station utilization.
- Monitor the operational status of bike-sharing stations.
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard for operational insights.
- Support data-driven decisions for bike redistribution and capacity planning.

## 🔍 Key Insights
### Descriptive Analytics
- Most stations remain operational across the network.
- Bike availability varies significantly between cities.
- Several cities contain a large concentration of bike-sharing stations.
### Diagnostic Analytics
- High-utilization stations indicate strong commuter demand.
- Underutilized stations may have excess capacity or low rider traffic.
- Bike availability differs across geographic regions.
### Predictive Analytics
- High-demand stations may require additional bike capacity.
- Growing urban areas may need network expansion.
- Stations with frequent shortages may experience future demand pressure.
### Prescriptive Analytics
- Improve bike redistribution between stations.
- Increase capacity at consistently busy locations.
- Optimize underutilized stations.
- Prioritize maintenance and reopening of closed stations.
## ✅ Conclusion
The Bike Sharing Station Analysis highlights station utilization, bike availability, and network performance across multiple cities. The analysis identified highly utilized stations, underutilized resources, and variations in bike availability across locations. These insights help optimize bike redistribution, improve station capacity planning, and enhance operational efficiency while supporting data-driven decision-making.
